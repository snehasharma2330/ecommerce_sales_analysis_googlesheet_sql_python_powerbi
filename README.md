
E-Commerce Sales Analysis Report

## PROJECT OVERVIEW
### This project analyzes an E-Commerce Sales dataset to uncover valuable business insights related to sales performance, profitability, customer purchasing behaviour, product performance and regional trends. The project follows a complete analysis workflow including data cleaning, SQL analysis, Python exploratory data analysis (EDA) and an interactive dashboard with KPIs.
---
 ## OBJECTIVES
### •	Clean and prepare raw e-commerce sales data.
### •	Analyse sales and profit performance across different regions and product categories.
### •	Identify top-performing products and customers data.
### •	Evaluate payment methods and discount patterns.
### •	Build an interactive dashboard to support business decision-making.
---

## Key Insights
-	The highest revenue was generated by the top-performing region, highlighting strong regional demand.
-	A small group of products contributed a significant share of total profit.
-	Digital payment methods accounted for a large portion of sales transactions.
-	Certain sub-categories consistently outperformed other in revenue generation.
-	Some product categories maintained higher profit margins despite offering discounts.
-	Monthly sales showed noticeable fluctuations, indication seasonal buying patterns.
---

## BUSINESS RECOMMENDATION
-	Focus marketing efforts on the highest-performing regions.
-	Increase inventory for top-selling products to avoid stock shortages.
-	Review discount patterns for low-profit products.
-	Develop loyalty programs targeting high-value customers.
-	Promote high-margin product categories through targeted campaigns.
-	Encourage the use of preferred payment methods to improve customer convenience.
-	Monitor monthly sales trends for better inventory planning.
